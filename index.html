Who we are

Not a trader.
Not a buying agent.
Your team in Asia.

Spend Matters is an apparel sourcing and supply-chain company. Brands and retailers hire us to be their sourcing office in Asia — we select factories, negotiate prices, develop products, watch quality and get goods shipped on time.

What makes us different is how we work: every order runs on our AI platform. Twelve software agents track approvals, samples, compliance certificates and shipments around the clock, and flag risk before it becomes a delay. You get the judgement of people who have spent 20 years on factory floors, backed by software that never sleeps.
