Global sourcing

Global sourcing
without guesswork.

Finding a factory is the easy part. The work is knowing which factory can hold your price, your quality and your delivery at the same time — and staying on it after the order is placed.

Before a single supplier is contacted we study what you are actually buying — the product and its construction, the fabric, the target FOB, quantity, delivery window, quality expectation and compliance requirement. Where you have history with the style or with previous suppliers, that history is part of the brief.

CategoryConstructionFabricTarget FOBQuantityDeliveryQuality levelComplianceSupplier history

We compare capable factories across our qualified regions — India, Bangladesh and China — and, where a product calls for it, look further. The objective is not the cheapest quotation. It is the right balance of price, product capability, quality, compliance, free capacity, lead time, past performance and how well the factory communicates when something goes wrong.

A factory enters your programme only after it is qualified: ownership and history, experience with comparable customers, audit and compliance status, product capability, real production capacity, internal quality systems, social compliance and commercial reliability. What we learn is recorded, so the next enquiry starts from evidence rather than memory.

Quality & inspection

Independent inspection.
Exactly to your standard.

Controlling quality in Asia should not require you to keep a buying agent or a liaison office of your own. Our inspection team works to your specification, your checkpoints and your acceptance level — and reports to you, not to the factory.

Licensed programmes

Licence workflows, tracked like any other requirement.

Licensed product carries its own layer of approvals — artwork, placement, sampling and documentation, each with its own deadline. Where you work on licensed programmes, those requirements can be tracked alongside the rest of the order.

  • Licence requirements per style and market
  • Approval status and outstanding submissions
  • Artwork and placement follow-up
  • Supplier compliance against licensor terms
  • Required documentation register
  • Expiry and renewal dates

Spend Matters does not grant, issue or represent any licence. Where a customer holds licences — for example character or brand programmes such as Disney, Marvel, Smiley or FAMA — we help track the approvals, artwork and documentation those programmes require.

Testing & report intelligence

Turn thousands of PDFs
into decisions.

Lab reports, fabric and garment testing, chemical and performance results, customer-specific protocols — they arrive as documents and are read by a person who has forty other things to do.

This is document intelligence and workflow automation, not a laboratory opinion. The result of a test is the laboratory's; what we do is make sure the right person sees it in time.

  • Read reports as they arrive, in the format they arrive in
  • Extract the results that matter to your requirement
  • Identify pass and fail status against the defined standard
  • Compare results with the customer's specification
  • Flag missing tests before the shipment is booked
  • Track retesting through to a closed result
  • Alert the responsible team, not a shared mailbox

Shipment intelligence

From container booking to arrival.
Stay ahead of the ETA.

The order is finished, the risk is not. Most delivery surprises are visible days before anyone notices them — in a booking that slipped, a vessel that was changed or an ETA that quietly moved.

  1. Booking
  2. Forwarder
  3. Container
  4. Vessel
  5. Port
  6. Transit
  7. ETA

What is followed

  • Container follow-up against the booking
  • Vessel booking confirmation
  • Forwarder follow-up and correspondence
  • Shipment status by order and style
  • Vessel tracking through third-party tracking services
  • ETA monitoring and change alerts
  • Exception alerts when something moves

Why it matters

The value is not a map. It is that your team stops opening five carrier sites every morning to find out that nothing changed — and hears about it the day it does.

Vessel and container tracking uses third-party data and services, and its accuracy depends on those sources. Alerts are decision support for your team, not a guarantee of carrier performance.
